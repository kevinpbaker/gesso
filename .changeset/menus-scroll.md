`Menu` and `MenuBar` panels scroll when they are longer than the room they are given. A menu bar's thirty-item Format menu in a short window used to run off the bottom, with nothing past the edge reachable; it now stops at the window's edge and its rows scroll inside the frame with the wheel, while the arrow keys, Home and End keep the highlighted row in view. Hovering a row does not scroll the list.
