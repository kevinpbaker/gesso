`touchEmulation` takes a mouse over a subtree as a finger, as a browser's device mode does: with nothing pressed it hovers nothing there, and a press there is a finger's from down to up (its slop, its gestures, its hover dropped when it lifts). For a design tool playing a screen drawn for touch with a mouse. A finger, and a mouse anywhere else, are unchanged.
