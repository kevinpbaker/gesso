A length that is not one (`width: '100%'`) is refused where it is written, naming the element, instead of throwing in layout: that throw abandoned every frame, so one element's mistake stopped the whole application drawing. One that arrives bound, from an Observable, is reported once and laid out as if unset. `renderRoot(...).onError(listener)` hears every error the render worker reports to the shell, for an application that shows its errors itself with the plugin's `overlay: false`.
