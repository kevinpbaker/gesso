`Tour` takes `modal: false` for a tutorial that has the person do each step: the card takes the keyboard as a step opens, but the target can take it from there, so a field in the lit hole can be typed in and a control that opens something by taking focus opens it. Presses outside the hole are still the dim's. The default, `modal: true`, keeps the keyboard in the card as before.
